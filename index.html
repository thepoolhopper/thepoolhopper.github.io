About Pool Hopper

Pool Hopper is the ultimate mobile field service assistant built specifically for pool cleaning and maintenance professionals. Track chemical levels and dosing, log service visits, generate professional PDF invoices with photo proof of work, optimize daily GPS routes, and manage recurring client visits — all offline-first.

Getting Started Guide

  1. Set Up Your Defaults: Open Settings to set your default hourly rate and currency.
  2. Add Your Customers: Head to Job Entry or Customers tab to save customer pool addresses and contact info.
  3. Add Your Equipment: Register your pool vacuums, filter pumps, salt chlorine cells, and test kits under the Equipment tab.
  4. Log Service Visits: Record completed jobs — water chemical test results, skim & vacuum, shock treatments — with before & after photos.
  5. Generate Invoices & Run Reports: Issue PDF invoices directly to clients and track profit & loss from the Reports tab.
