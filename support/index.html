Frequently asked questions

How do I set my default hourly rate and currency?

Go to Settings and set your default hourly rate and preferred currency. These pre-fill every new job automatically, saving time in the field.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the list, or choose "System default" to follow your device's language automatically.

How do I add a new customer?

Open New Job, tap the customer field, and choose "Add New Customer." You only need to do this once — they'll appear in every future job. For fuller details (address, email, phone, default rate) manage customers from the Invoices tab.

How do I add a pool robot or piece of equipment?

Go to the Equipment & Maintenance tab and tap the + button. Add your pool vacuums, robotic cleaners, filter pumps, salt chlorine cells, and test kits. The Pool Hopper automatically tracks filter hours and motor hours as you log jobs, and alerts you when service is due.

How do I track filter hours and set a service interval?

When adding or editing equipment, set the Filter interval (hrs) field. Once the robot has run that many hours since its last filter replacement, the app shows a "Filter due" reminder on your dashboard and equipment card. After you log a Filter Replacement service event, the counter resets to zero automatically.

How do I log a pool service visit?

Tap New Job, select the customer and service date, choose the activities performed (chemical test, skim & vacuum, shock, backwash, etc.), add the hours, and optionally attach before/after photos. Tap Save — the job is instantly available for invoicing.

How do I log a filter or motor service?

Go to Equipment & Maintenance, find your robot, and tap Log Service. Select Filter Replacement (automatically resets filter hours) or the relevant task for your service. Add any parts used, the service date, and save.

How do I generate and send a PDF invoice?

Open the Invoices tab, create or find the invoice, then tap Export PDF. You can share it directly via email, AirDrop, or any app on your device.

How do I optimise my daily route?

On the Schedule tab, once a day has two or more jobs with a location saved, a Route order toggle appears for that day. Turn it on to reorder those jobs by shortest distance between them, with a number badge showing each stop's place in the sequence.

What does The Pool Hopper Pro include?

Pro unlocks unlimited jobs and customers, PDF invoice generation, route optimisation, equipment maintenance tracking with automatic hour resets, photo attachments, expense tracking, profit & loss reports, automatic backups, multi-device sync, a job timer, and per-activity checklists.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device (offline-first). Pro users can turn on Automatic Backups (Settings → Automatic backups) to create a dated backup file on a schedule you choose, which you can save to iCloud Drive. We also recommend enabling iCloud Backup on your iPhone so your data is included in Apple's standard device backup.

How do I sync data between two devices?

Go to Settings → Multi-device sync (Pro) and tap Save a sync file. Store it in iCloud Drive, Files, or any cloud-storage app, then open that same file from your other device's Settings → Multi-device sync → Load & merge. The Pool Hopper merges the two devices' data automatically — whichever side has the newest change to a job, customer, invoice, expense, or piece of equipment wins, so nothing recent is lost. There's no account, password, or always-on server involved — it all travels through a file you control.

How does the Job Timer work?

Pro users can turn on the Job Timer in Settings → General settings. Tap Start Timer on the Dashboard when you begin a job — it keeps tracking even if you switch apps. Tap Stop and the elapsed time is rounded up to whichever increment you've set under "Round time to" (5, 10, or 15 minutes, or Off), then dropped straight into a new Job Entry ready to save.

What are per-activity checklists?

When you choose one or more activities for a job (chemical test, skim & vacuum, shock, backwash, etc.), The Pool Hopper Pro automatically attaches a checklist of the typical steps for those activities to the Job Entry screen. Check items off as you complete them in the field — your progress is saved with the job.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data. You'll be asked to confirm twice, since this can't be undone. It permanently removes every job, customer, invoice, expense, equipment record, and photo from this device — a sync file you've already saved elsewhere, or another device's own copy, is unaffected.
