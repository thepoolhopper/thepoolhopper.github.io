Frequently asked questions

How do I set my default hourly rate and currency?

Go to Settings and set your default hourly rate and preferred currency. These pre-fill every new job automatically, saving time in the field.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the list, or choose "System default" to follow your device's language automatically.

How do I add a new customer?

Open New Job, tap the customer field, and choose "Add New Customer." You only need to do this once — they'll appear in every future job. For fuller details (address, email, phone, default rate) manage customers from the Invoices tab.

How do I add a pool robot or piece of equipment?

Go to the Equipment & Maintenance tab and tap the + button. Add your pool vacuums, robotic cleaners, filter pumps, salt chlorine cells, and test kits. The Pool Hopper automatically tracks filter hours and motor hours as you log jobs, and alerts you when service is due.

How do I track filter hours and set a service interval?

When adding or editing equipment, set the Filter interval (hrs) field. Once the robot has run that many hours since its last filter replacement, the app shows a "Filter due" reminder on your dashboard and equipment card. After you log a Filter Replacement service event, the counter resets to zero automatically.

How do I log a pool service visit?

Tap New Job, select the customer and service date, choose the activities performed (chemical test, skim & vacuum, shock, backwash, etc.), add the hours, and optionally attach before/after photos. Tap Save — the job is instantly available for invoicing.

How do I log a filter or motor service?

Go to Equipment & Maintenance, find your robot, and tap Log Service. Select Filter Replacement (automatically resets filter hours) or the relevant task for your service. Add any parts used, the service date, and save.

How do I generate and send a PDF invoice?

Open the Invoices tab, create or find the invoice, then tap Export PDF. You can share it directly via email, AirDrop, or any app on your device.

How do I optimise my daily route?

In the Route tab, add your stops for the day and tap Optimise Route to reorder by shortest drive time. Each stop opens Maps navigation with one tap.

What does The Pool Hopper Pro include?

Pro unlocks unlimited jobs and customers, PDF invoice generation, route optimisation, equipment maintenance tracking with automatic hour resets, photo attachments, expense tracking, profit & loss reports, and Household Cloud Sync across devices.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device (offline-first). Enable Household Cloud Sync (Pro feature) to sync across multiple devices. We also recommend enabling iCloud backup on your iPhone so your data is included in Apple's standard device backup.
