Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The Pool Hopper ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the data you handle when using our mobile application.

1. Local, Offline-First Data Storage

The Pool Hopper is designed as an offline-first application. All customer details, service visit entries, equipment logs, chemical dosing records, PDF invoices, and attached photos remain stored locally on your device.

2. Optional Cloud Synchronisation

If you choose to enable Household Cloud Sync (a Pro feature), your job, expense, and equipment records are synchronised securely using end-to-end device linking. We do not sell, rent, or share your business data with third parties.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

The Pool Hopper requests access to your device's camera and photo library solely to allow you to attach before/after service photos and expense receipt photos. These photos remain strictly on your local device unless included in PDF invoices you export.

4. Location Services

Location permissions are used strictly on-demand to assist in auto-filling job site addresses and optimising daily route sequences. Location data is never broadcast or sold.

5. In-App Purchases

In-app purchase transactions for The Pool Hopper Pro are processed securely through Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment details or credit card information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@beltov.dk.
